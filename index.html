Welcome

Welcome to Chatuge Shores

Located on the shores of Lake Chatuge and tucked away in the beautiful North Carolina mountains, Chatuge Shores is a semi-private facility offering one of the best golfing experiences in the area.

Measuring 6,498 yards from the back tees down to 5,051 for seniors, the course offers a genuine challenge for players of all skill levels — wrapped in some of the most scenic mountain and lakeside views you'll find on a round of golf.
