About the Course

About Chatuge Shores

Located on the shores of Lake Chatuge and tucked away in the beautiful North Carolina mountains, Chatuge Shores is a semi-private facility offering one of the best golfing experiences in the area.

Measuring 6,498 yards from the back tees down to 5,051 for seniors, the course offers a genuine challenge for players of all skill levels. Four sets of tees mean every golfer can play a course that fits their game.

Practice facilities include a driving range, putting green and pro shop — everything you need to warm up before your round or sharpen your game.

Course Layout & Rules

Get to know all 18 holes before you play, and review our course rules for a smooth, enjoyable round.

Course Rules

All play governed by U.S.G.A. Rules, unless otherwise covered by local rules.

  1. Play must be completed in 4 1/4 hours.
  2. If ball is lost, signal other matches to pass you.
  3. Keep carts on paths at tees & greens.
  4. Keep carts on paths at par 3's behind all ropes.
  5. Only two riders per cart.
  6. No one under 16 can operate a cart.
  7. Replace all divots & repair ball marks.
  8. Smooth sand in traps.
  9. No more than four players in any group.
  10. Shirts must be worn on the course.
  11. All players must register.
  12. Always allow faster players through.
  13. Keep carts out of woods.
  14. No children under 6 allowed on course.
  15. Every player must have his own clubs.
  16. Carts use 90 degree rule.
