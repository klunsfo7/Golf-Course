Director of Golf / General Manager

William “Bill” Rinaldo

PGA Life Member

Originally from northern New York, Bill moved south in 1981 and settled in Zephyrhills, Florida, where he served as the Pro/Manager of the city golf course for seven and a half years. In 1988 he designed and built Scotland Yards Golf Course in Dade City, Florida, where he remained until it was sold in 2007.

A life member of the PGA, Bill spent a 25-year period serving the North Florida PGA Section as Junior Chairman, Senior Chairman and Section Tournament Chairman before retiring in 2007. He then worked part time at Chatuge until he was called into service when the Pro/Manager position opened.

Bill is also an accomplished musician and has performed across the Southeast. He has three children and nine grandchildren, all of whom live in Florida.
